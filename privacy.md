# ChordKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publishing]

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use ChordKeep. The short version: **your songs stay on your device, and we never see them.**

## Your songs are yours

Everything you create or import in ChordKeep is stored only on your device. We don't collect, receive, store or sell any of it. There is no ChordKeep account and no ChordKeep cloud.

## No telemetry

ChordKeep doesn't send anything about you or how you use the app. There are no analytics, no crash reports, no usage tracking and no advertising IDs. The app doesn't need an internet connection to work.

## Camera, photos and files

- **Camera and photos.** When you take or pick a photo to import, ChordKeep reads it on your device. The photo is never uploaded anywhere.
- **Files.** When you import a file, ChordKeep reads it on your device. The file is never uploaded anywhere.

ChordKeep only uses the camera, photos or files when you choose to import something.

## Backups

Your device may back up ChordKeep's data to the backup service you use, such as Google's Android backup, iCloud, or your own Windows backup. That backup is between you and that service, under their privacy terms. We have no access to it.

## Sharing and exporting

When you share or export from ChordKeep, it creates a file and hands it to your device's share panel or saves it where you choose. It goes only to the people and apps you pick. We are not involved and don't receive a copy.

## Taking your data with you

You can export your data as standard files at any time, with no account, fee or waiting.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a song in ChordKeep removes it from your device. Uninstalling ChordKeep removes all of its data from your device. Copies in your own backups, or files you've shared or exported, are kept or deleted by you.

## Children

ChordKeep doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. ChordKeep will never start collecting your data without asking you first.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: support@chordkeep.com
